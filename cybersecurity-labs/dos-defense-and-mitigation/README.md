# DoS Defense and Mitigation

## Purpose

Document defensive resilience concepts for denial-of-service scenarios in a safe, authorized learning environment.

## Suggested contents

- `notes.md` — Threat model, lab scope, and observations.
- `evidence/` — Sanitized metrics, architecture diagrams, or logs.
- `mitigations.md` — Rate limiting, capacity planning, WAF/CDN options, monitoring, incident response, and recovery planning.

## Safety

Do not conduct traffic-generation or availability testing against public services or systems without explicit written authorization and defined testing boundaries.
